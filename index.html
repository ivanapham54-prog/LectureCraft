Evolving OpenAI Academy into an AI-Native Higher Ed Community

Make it easier to discover, learn, and collaborate, powered by AI and peer expertise.

CURRENT GAPS

Limited visible discussions

Conversations are hard to find and not front and center.

No conversational AI embedded

Users cannot ask questions and get AI-powered answers from the community's knowledge.

Over-reliance on long-form webinars

60+ minute sessions are hard to consume and do not fit busy schedules.

Feels like a content hub, not a community

More content consumption than active participation and peer learning.

MY SOLUTION
Easy for everyone to use
Faculty
Staff
Students

1. Conversational AI (Embedded)

ChatGPT Edu assistant lets users ask questions, get synthesized answers, and take action faster.

Answer questions Synthesize insights Recommend next steps

2. Community with Prompts

Spark peer participation with prompt-led discussion spaces where members can share what is working, compare approaches, and build on each other's examples.

3. Bite-Sized Learning

Curated 4-8 minute clips from webinars and events make learning digestible, role-relevant, and actionable. Tagged by role to surface what matters most.

THE IMPACT

Faster AI adoption

Easier access to proven strategies and real campus examples.

Higher engagement & participation

Relevant content and AI-powered guidance drive active community involvement.

Stronger peer learning network

Institutions learn from each other and scale what works.

Scalable knowledge base

AI-powered discovery creates a living, growing repository of insights.

From a content hub to an AI-native community where higher ed leaders connect, learn, and lead.
